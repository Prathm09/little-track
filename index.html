Little Track is an academic prototype. Image-based estimates are not a diagnosis and must not replace laboratory bilirubin testing or a qualified clinician's assessment.

Seek urgent medical care if a newborn develops jaundice in the first 24 hours, is difficult to wake, feeds poorly, or has other concerning symptoms.

Little Track guide

Quick user manual.

A simple guide to the academic prototype and its current limitations.

Parent / guardian

  1. Open Profile and choose Parent / Guardian.
  2. Enter demo profile details and accept the prototype consent statement.
  3. Choose Upload Photo or Use Camera. Allow camera permission if prompted.
  4. Preview the image before submitting a demo record.
  5. Review the local record list. The current demo does not send an SMS or contact a doctor.

Doctor

  1. Open Profile and switch to Doctor.
  2. Review demo submissions visible in this browser.
  3. In production, doctor access must be authenticated and restricted to assigned patients.

Camera troubleshooting

  • Camera access generally requires HTTPS or localhost and browser permission.
  • Close other applications using the camera and try again.
  • For gallery uploads, use a JPG or PNG image.

Technical integration

ESP32-CAM streaming, backend image processing, a trained ML model, secure database, and SMS notifications are not connected in this starter website. These require separate configuration and testing.

Medical safety: do not use this prototype to diagnose or rule out jaundice. Follow your clinician's advice and appropriate bilirubin testing.
